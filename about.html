Who We Are

Built on experience.

Green Checkered Pants is a two-person consultancy co-founded by Scott and Tammy Smith. Between us we bring over fifty years of combined professional experience spanning enterprise software, data engineering, Medicare operations, and business management.

Company Values

We deliver honest assessments, transparent pricing, and solutions that prioritize our clients' long-term success over short-term gains.

We build solutions designed to last — intuitive, maintainable, and easy to hand off.

We apply industry standards and best practices as appropriate to each implementation, ensuring our work is sound, defensible, and built to endure.

We value and carefully consider all client input and feedback — it is essential to delivering outcomes that truly serve the business.

We are committed to open, direct communication and expect the same in return. Clarity at every stage leads to better outcomes for everyone.

We are selective about the clients we take on so that we can bring a high level of attention and care to every engagement, ensuring the best possible outcome for all parties.
